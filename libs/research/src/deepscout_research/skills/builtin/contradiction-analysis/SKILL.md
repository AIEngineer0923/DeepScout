# Contradiction analysis

1. Record both sides with snapshot-backed quotes.
2. Do not resolve a contradiction by deleting evidence.
3. Do not treat disagreement as automatic human-review.
4. Leave unresolved conflicts explicit for the critic/synthesis engines.
